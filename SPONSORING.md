# Sponsoring SneppX

SneppX is an open-core organization. The MIT core is free forever; your support
funds maintenance, security work, and the documentation.

Sponsor buttons are enabled on all public repos and route to the account that
runs this work. Tiers below are suggestions - every contribution counts.

## Tiers

- **Coffee** ($5/mo) - you keep the core healthy. Listed as a supporter in the
  org profile README.
- **Supporter** ($25/mo) - priority answers on repo issues + your name/brand in
  the org profile README.
- **License** ($99/mo) - commercial-use license acknowledgement for SneppX
  open-core components in commercial products, plus quarterly office hours.
- **Enterprise** (custom) - priority support, private channels, sponsored
  features, and consulting for your AI-security/distributed-training needs.

## Where the money goes

1. Maintenance of `sneppx-alg` (security fixes, audit follow-ups).
2. Documentation, learning material, and worked examples for the framework.
3. Compute for CI and releases.

## What's Been Built with Sponsor Support

## Sponsorship FAQ

### Is `sneppx-alg` free for commercial use?
Yes. The core library is licensed under the permissive MIT License, permitting commercial integration, modification, and redistribution.

### What is included in the Enterprise sponsorship tier?
Enterprise sponsors receive private communication channels, SLA-backed response times, architectural reviews for custom deployments, and sponsored feature prioritisation.

### How can I request an invoice or alternative payment method?
Open a private security advisory or contact `algoSNEPPX@gmail.com` for corporate invoicing, procurement details, or tailored service agreements.
